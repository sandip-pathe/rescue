Privacy Policy for Blissm

Last updated: April 9, 2025

Welcome to Blissm, a mental wellness app designed to support users with AI-powered journaling, CBT tools, and community interaction. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

🔍 Information We Collect

1. Personal Information

  • Email (optional): If you sign up or contact support.
  • Camera Access: Only used when users choose to upload or share images. We do not access your camera otherwise.

2. Non-Personal Data

  • Usage analytics (e.g., session duration, clicks)
  • Device information (OS, model)

3. Sensitive User Data

  • Journal entries and chats are stored locally and are not collected.
  • Data may be used anonymously to improve AI suggestions.

🤖 How We Use Your Information

  • To personalize your mental health journey
  • To enhance AI responses (with consent)
  • To improve app performance and detect issues

🔒 Data Security

  • All sensitive data is stored securely using encryption.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data.

🧠 Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services like:

  • Firebase (Authentication, Storage)
  • OpenAI for chatbot functionality

These services follow industry-standard security practices.

👤 Your Rights

  • Request deletion of your data anytime
  • Opt-out of analytics in settings
  • Contact us for privacy concerns

📝 Children’s Privacy

Blissm is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
